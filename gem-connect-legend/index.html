🎮 HOW TO PLAY MANUAL

1. Selection: Click or tap on any cell on the grid to select a gem. The chosen gem will be highlighted with a yellow border.
2. Match-3 Mechanic: Select exactly 3 identical gems from anywhere on the board. They do not need to be adjacent or connected in a straight line.
3. Scoring System: Successfully matching 3 identical gems will clear them from the board, yield new random gems, and award you +10 points. Selecting mismatched gems will reset your current selection.
4. Time Constraint: You only have a strict limit of 60 seconds to complete matches. Think fast, clear boards rapidly, and defeat your personal High Score before the countdown hits zero!
